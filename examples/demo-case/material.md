## Paper metadata

Synthetic paper "Cycling stability of porous carbon electrodes", DOI 10.5555/demo.2024.009, authors Li Wei and Zhang Min, year 2024, version 1.0, license CC-BY-4.0.

## Results

At 1 A/g, the PC-A electrode retained 92 % of its initial capacitance after 5000 cycles, whereas the activated carbon baseline retained only 78 % under the same conditions.

In a separate organic-electrolyte test at 2 A/g, PC-B retained 85 % of its initial capacitance after 5000 cycles.

## Interpretation

The higher retention of PC-A suggests that the resorcinol-derived pore structure is more stable than the activated carbon baseline.
